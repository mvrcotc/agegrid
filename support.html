Support

Your life, arranged by how old you were

Write to support@agegrid.app and you will get a reply from a person. You can also reach us from inside the app, under Settings › Contact the team, which attaches your app and iOS version so we can skip the first three emails.

My grid is empty

Age Grid needs permission to read your photo library. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Age Grid and choose Full Access. Limited access means the app only sees the photos you individually picked, which usually makes for a very empty grid.

The years look wrong

Check your date of birth in Settings. Everything in Age Grid is measured from it, so a wrong birthday shifts the whole grid. Changing it re-sorts every photo and alters nothing in your library.

A photo is on the wrong day

Open the photo and tap the date to correct it. By default the correction lives inside Age Grid and your photo library is left alone. If you want the fix written back to Photos itself, turn on Settings › Save corrections to Photos — iOS will ask you to confirm each change.

Hiding versus deleting

Hiding takes a photo off your grid and leaves it in your library; hidden photos are listed under Settings › Hidden photos, and one tap puts any of them back. Deleting removes the photo from your library and sends it to Recently Deleted in Photos, where it stays for 30 days.

Do I need an account?

No. Every feature works signed out. Signing in with Apple only names the private iCloud record set that syncs your corrections between your own devices — it never uploads a photograph.

Deleting your account

Settings › Account › Delete account signs you out and erases everything Age Grid stored in your iCloud. Your photos and the corrections on your phone are untouched.

Still stuck? support@agegrid.app — tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead, and we will get back to you.
