Age Grid

Your life, one year at a time

An iPhone app

Your photo library is the most complete record of your life that has ever existed. It is also sorted by nothing more useful than the date. Age Grid re-indexes the photos already on your phone by how old you were when you took them — one hundred squares, one for each year of a life.

Age Grid never copies your photographs and never uploads them. It reads them through Apple’s Photos framework and shows them to you. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.
