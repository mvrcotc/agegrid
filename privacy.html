Age Grid

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Age Grid reads the photos already on your phone and arranges them by how old you were when you took them. It does not upload them, copy them, or send them anywhere. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your date of birth. Your corrections — dates, places, notes, ordering, and which photos you have hidden. Your milestones. Your profile picture. All of it lives in this app's own storage on your phone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Your photographs are never copied into Age Grid. The app reads them from your photo library through Apple's Photos framework and shows them to you.

Locking the app

You can require Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode before Age Grid will open. Age Grid never asks for a password of its own and stores no passcode, PIN, or biometric data: it asks iOS to confirm it is you, and iOS answers yes or no. Your face and fingerprint never leave Apple's Secure Enclave and are never seen by this app. While the lock is on, your photographs are also hidden from the iOS app switcher.

What leaves your device, and only if you ask

  • iCloud sync, if you turn it on, stores your corrections and milestones in your own private iCloud database. That is your Apple account, not ours — we cannot read it. No photographs are ever synced, only your notes and corrections about them.
  • Saving a correction back to Photos, if you turn it on, changes the date or location on the photo in your own library. iOS asks you to confirm each time.
  • A backup file, if you export one, goes wherever you choose to put it.
  • Looking up place names. Opening a photo, or the Places screen, asks Apple to turn that location into a place name — the city and country a photograph was taken in. Only a coordinate is sent — never a photograph, a date, or anything identifying you — and it is rounded first: to about a kilometre when naming where one photograph was taken, and to about ten when grouping your library by country. Every answer is kept on your phone, so each area is looked up once and works offline after that. This is Apple’s own geocoding service, covered by Apple’s privacy policy; we never see it.
  • An email to support goes to us, and includes only what you write plus your app version, iOS version and device model.

Signing in

Signing in is optional and only names the iCloud record set that syncs between your own devices. Every feature works without an account.

Age Grid uses Sign in with Apple and never sees a password. The only thing stored is the anonymous identifier Apple returns, kept in the device Keychain and excluded from backups. Deleting your account from inside the app removes that identifier and erases the synced record; your photos and the corrections on your phone are untouched.

The camera

A photo you take inside Age Grid is written to your own photo library, exactly as if you had used the Camera app. It is not sent anywhere and Age Grid keeps no separate copy.

Children

Age Grid is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

You can revoke photo access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos. You can export or erase your Age Grid data from within the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device; deleting your account removes the synced copy too.

Questions about any of this: support@agegrid.app
